# Bestpresso v0.1.31 — Localisation support

- Choose English, German, or follow your device language in Settings → App experience.
- Localised labels, dates, numbers, and stage messages throughout Bestpresso, while keeping your profile names and saved data unchanged.
- A shared translation foundation makes future languages and English copy updates easier to maintain.
- Smoother profile browsing and less repeated formatting work on lower-end devices.
- Improved spacing for the steam “Off” label, including German “Aus”.

Thanks to Tobias Gregorius for the German translation and performance contributions in PRs #84 and #85.
