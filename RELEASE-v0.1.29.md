# Bestpresso v0.1.29 — Your profile library, redesigned

## Browse your recipes

- A new profile library brings the familiar Settings and Insights sidebar to profile browsing.
- Search by name, category, description, or author; filter by category and source; sort alphabetically or by recently added.
- See each recipe’s pressure and flow preview, dose, target yield, and temperature directly in the list.

## Make favorites yours

- Compact favorite cards use stars and clear Unfavorite actions, without slot numbers or selected-profile badges.
- Reorder favorites with circular up/down controls. The saved order carries through to your home cover flow.
- When all five slots are full, choose which favorite to replace. The replaced recipe stays in your library.

## A clearer profile detail

- A full-width graph header puts the recipe first, with a softly shaded title and action bar.
- Target metrics overlap the graph by 12px. Profile information and the description sit below in a one-third/two-thirds layout, stacking on phones.
- Select, favorite, edit, and delete remain connected to your actual Decaid profiles. Delete stays rightmost in red, with confirmation and existing protections for built-in and currently selected profiles.
- Close returns to your filtered list, keeping browsing and recipe detail separate.

## Create and import

- Create a profile or import a JSON recipe directly from the library, then review it in the existing editor.
- Imported recipes can now be saved without making an unnecessary edit. Duplicate import names are handled automatically.
- New profiles record whether they were created or imported. Older recipes without that information appear as Saved rather than being guessed into a source category.

The redesigned screens follow your light/dark theme, temperature unit, and chart line-weight preferences. Light mode remains Beta; dark remains the default. Existing profiles, favorites, shot history, and brewing behavior are preserved.

## Patch 001 — Hot water & steam

- Redesigned expanded hot-water and steam cards with a cleaner layout and a circular steam-temperature indicator.
- Adjust temperature, amount, flow, and maximum duration through the existing controls, with values kept in sync with Settings.
- Smoother temperature updates, steam on/off transitions, and expand/collapse animations. Familiar compact summaries remain unchanged.
- Improved collapsed-card spacing on shorter screens and fixed Fahrenheit adjustments rounding above the temperature limit.

Reservoir, scale, profiles, insights, and history remain unchanged by this patch. Both dark and Light Beta themes are supported.

Install `bestpresso-v0.1.29-patch001.zip` in Decaid.

## Patch 002 — More comfortable utility cards

- Better spacing on narrower screens, with a full-width steam-temperature gauge above Flow and Max duration.
- A true semicircular steam gauge frees up space, with rebalanced Hot water and Steam card heights and roomier title areas.
- Simplified Hot water to Temperature and Volume on the home screen. Max duration remains available in Settings and still applies when dispensing.
- Refined spacing for Scale, Insights, and Last shot on smaller tablets, while preserving smooth expand/collapse and temperature transitions.

Install `bestpresso-v0.1.29-patch002.zip` in Decaid.
